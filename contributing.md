# Contributing

Thanks for helping make this list better. Awesome Muse is **curated, not
collected**: we add fewer things than we are sent, on purpose. A short list of
great links is more useful than a long list of okay ones. It's a toolbox,
not a newspaper: we favor things that give the reader something to *do*
(build, connect, try) over things to *know*.

## What belongs here

Resources about **Muse, Meta's personal AI agent**, that help people use it
well:

- Official resources from Meta.
- Real use cases, practical guides, and tips.
- Connectors, skills, and open-source tools built for Muse.
- Independent reviews, security research, and original reporting.
- Talks, interviews, and podcasts.
- News only when it changes what a reader can do with Muse: launches, new capabilities, availability or policy changes. Milestones and download counts age out in weeks.

Out of scope: the Muse Spark and Muse Glimmer models, Muse Code, the Meta Model
API, and anything else named "Muse".

## Quality bar

A resource is added only if it:

- Is about Muse the assistant, and works or reads as described.
- Adds something the list does not already have.
- Is original: not a rewrite of Meta's announcement or another article.
- Vendor-made resources are judged by the same bar: a product pitch inside does not disqualify it if the resource is genuinely useful on its own and the pitch is disclosed and secondary.
- For projects: has a README and a license, and a commit in the last 90 days.

We do not accept:

- Referral or affiliate links.
- Tools that break Meta's terms of service.
- SEO "alternatives" pages or competitor marketing.
- List-of-lists and competitor catalogs: list the thing itself, not someone else's list.
- Low-effort, AI-generated content.

## How to submit

The easiest way is to [open a submission issue][new-issue]. Pull requests are
welcome too:

- One resource per pull request.
- Use the format `- [Name](url) - Description.`
- Keep the description to one objective line that starts with a capital letter
  and ends with a period. No hype words, no emoji.
- Add the entry at the end of the most fitting section, unless the section is
  sorted differently.
- Say so if you made the resource. Self-submissions are fine, but disclosure is
  required.

To report a broken link or outdated information, [open an issue][new-issue].

By contributing, you agree to the [Code of Conduct](code-of-conduct.md) and to
release your contribution under [CC0](license).

[new-issue]: https://github.com/cszach/awesome-muse/issues/new/choose
